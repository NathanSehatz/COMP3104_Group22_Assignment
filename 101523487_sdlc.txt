# On the Importance of DevOps in the software development lifecycle
## Or: How to make digital pearls
### Part 1
Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) is the process
of
-A) Providing a steady flow of code changes to more easily isolate 
buggy code, as opposed to uploading a large tangled knot of information 
that is harder to review;
-B) Deploying these reviewed and approved chunks of code in a seamless 
fashion, reducing downtime for the consumer/client of the operation.
